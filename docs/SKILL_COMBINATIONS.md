# Skill combinations

Version 0.14.0 adds 39 interactions after the first 25. The expanded source collection covers 107 distinct skills; together with the 13 original curated recipes there are now 77 combinations across 116 of the 399 source skills. Overlap is counted once. This remains partial coverage, without a full-build or endgame effectiveness rating.

## User flow

- Use **Browse skills with researched combinations** or the **Only skills with researched combinations** checkbox. Category and text search continue to apply. The filter is preserved in shared URLs.
- Each interaction appears from every participating skill. **Explore** opens the other member's catalog record.
- A first test is visible immediately. Conditions, mistakes to avoid, sources and the build-experiment checklist remain expandable.
- Skills without a researched recipe can expose **Other item experiments** from the existing mechanic engine. These remain separately labeled source candidates. The first two display their item modifiers and a practical test; further relationships open the source panel. Shared tags alone do not create a recipe.
- Item experiments are initially collapsed when researched recipes exist, preserving the simpler main layout.

## Evidence and counterchecks

Checked on 2026-10-15 against PathOfBuildingCommunity/PathOfBuilding-PoE2 revision `bb52d6b368307457eb9c54bb13f1829993d390b1`. The exact skill descriptions and record evidence are stored in `data/discovery/pob-bb52d6b-skill-combos2.json`. `npm run discovery:check -- PATH-TO-PINNED-POB` verifies each record fingerprint, line and description against that checkout. Each recipe links its actual source records. Neither Lua nor external instructions are executed.

The [official 0.5.0 notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540) were checked for changes affecting the proposed setup, particularly Toxic Growth limits, Tempest Bell activation frequency and Lightning Warp targeting fixes. Pinned descriptions remain the basis for the conditional interactions, rather than a claim of complete live-patch verification.

The second pass checked for mistaken automatic behavior and unavailable resources:

- Ball Lightning projectile targeting does not establish Lightning Warp effects that require an enemy target.
- Solar Orb moves Flameblast's centre but does not automate the channel. Skill acquisition must be checked on the actual loadout.
- Contagion spreading and Killing Palm charge generation require an actual death; they are not self-sustaining against an isolated boss.
- Profane Ritual needs an unconsumed corpse. Bonestorm's Impale payoff applies to later Attack hits, not its own spell hits.
- Shockwave Totem cannot create its own Jagged Ground. Magnetic Salvo spends its lingering objects.
- Ice Shot must actually Freeze; Snipe also needs Perfect Timing and a direct hit. Tempest Bell's hit interval prevents unlimited shockwave activation.
- Poison must reach the plant or pustules themselves. Poison on a nearby enemy is not sufficient evidence.
- Crossbow ammunition/reloading, grenade overlap and actual Detonator eligibility are part of the experiment. The Explosive Shot gem source includes the Detonator tag; the gem's loading effect and its separate attack effect are not flattened into one support profile.
- Added Fire from Flame Wall does not establish automatic Ignite on projectile targets.

No generic support gems, crit rates, mana values, damage multipliers or ascendancies were invented for the new interactions. Skills outside the small audited workbench use the source links and manual test plan; they do not silently enter the older support or trigger simulator.

## Validation boundary

Automated tests check reverse lookup, distinct coverage, source binding, complete test plans, unknown-record rejection, URL restoration, source-candidate labeling, integrity failures and mobile layout. These checks do not play the game. Actual timing, damage gain, resource sustain and endgame viability still require in-game observation.

## Second expansion review

The expansion preserves the original recipe file byte for byte and uses `pob-bb52d6b-skill-combos2` as its new identity. Old recipes are retained unchanged. Shared links explicitly naming the older collection fail with a version message rather than silently changing their evidence; opening the app at its base URL loads the current collection.

The second pass focused on scope and resource constraints:

- Offerings consume a Skeleton and depend on a living spike. Soul Offering improves the player's spells and explicitly does not buff minion damage; Bone Offering's Hit protection is not damage-over-time immunity.
- Armour Breaker/Sunder and Armour Piercing Rounds/High Velocity Rounds need Fully Broken Armour, then apply different named follow-up states. Obsolete consumption/automatic-crit assumptions were excluded.
- Pounce applies a socketed Mark instead of its default; Sniper's Mark still needs a later actual critical hit. No extra Mark-effect bonus is inferred from the old Pounce bug.
- Raging Spirits uses a manually cast Fire spell. The official 0.5.0 notes exclude the old totem/visage generation bugs. Firestorm's continuing bolts are not separate casts by the player.
- Oil Barrage's charge-funded mode does not make Oil Ground and consumes further Power Charges during continued channeling. Corpse or cull supply is not promised against an isolated boss.
- Furious Slam/Jagged Ground uses Shockwave Totem's explicit shapeshift compatibility. The source lists Talisman among its martial weapons; this is separate from the fixed bug allowing Shockwave Totem inside Ancestral Warrior Totem, which is not recommended.
- Abyssal Apparition's exclusions are retained: no channelling, cooldown, triggered, item-granted or already-charge-consuming spells. The proposed Spark cast is manual; no clone recursion is asserted.
- Ice Shot/Freezing Mark/Wave of Frost explicitly requires a bow-to-quarterstaff swap and an existing Primed-for-Freeze window; it is a more involved experiment, not a recommended leveling rotation.
- Ice Fragments and Ice Crystals remain separate object types. Fragmentation Rounds hits Glacial Bolt crystals; Cross Slash and Freezing Salvo consume fragments made by their matching generators.
- Volcano's initial channel bonus does not scale every later Slam-triggered spray. Tornado needs an actual Elemental Ground Surface; a burning enemy or Fire hit does not establish one.

The exact descriptions of all 107 sources are rechecked by the provenance command. Tests also verify unchanged prior recipes, reverse lookup for every member, unique recipe membership sets and new-skill coverage. None of these tests establish in-game damage, boss sustain or budget feasibility.
