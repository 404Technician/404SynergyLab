# Original artwork

The cinematic background is original generated environment art for the independent 404 Synergy Lab project. It is not an official Path of Exile asset, an item illustration or evidence of an in-game interaction.

- Project asset: `web/assets/sanctum-hero.webp`
- Generation mode: built-in imagegen tool; no external image service is used at runtime.
- Dimensions: 2172 × 724 pixels.
- Delivery: WebP encoded at quality 86, without cropping or resizing, approximately 234 KiB.
- Use: decorative hero background. The image has empty alternative text and is excluded from the accessibility tree; the headline and controls remain real HTML.
- The original SVG category art remains bundled and is distinct from exact game-item artwork.

## Generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: panoramic background artwork for an independent Path of Exile 2 build discovery website named 404 Synergy Lab. Create original cinematic dark fantasy environment concept art, wide landscape 3:1 composition. A vast ruined gothic sanctuary and ancient arcane forge, eroded stone arches, weathered dark bronze mechanisms, drifting grey mist, cinder sparks and subtle candlelight. The focal point is in the RIGHT THIRD: a tall ancient circular stone portal containing a fractured radiant ember-gold crystal with a restrained emerald core, suspended above a ritual dais, craggy roots and a few icy-blue crystalline shards below. Rich physically believable aged stone, oxidized bronze, deep charcoal, muted ochre light, low-saturation moss and ash; dramatic volumetric rays, film-like shadows, painted AAA dark fantasy game environment finish. LEFT HALF is quieter atmospheric ruined architecture and dark fog with low detail, suitable for large light-colored website headline overlay. Black edges fade naturally into darkness. Beautiful and mysterious, brutal grounded fantasy rather than science fiction. No people, no UI, no lettering, no text, no logos, no watermark, no border. Landscape artwork only, not a website mockup.
